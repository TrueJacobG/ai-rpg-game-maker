The lighthouse keeper, sixty, stubborn. She hid the lamp-room key and locked herself in the cellar after the lamp's lens cracked: she was too ashamed to call for help. She will give the key to anyone who is kind to her and not in a hurry. She is not dangerous, only proud and afraid of being blamed for a wreck.
